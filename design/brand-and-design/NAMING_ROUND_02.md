# Naming exploration — around Rally

September 18, 2026. Original creative proposals; no trademark, domain, handle or existing-product screening performed. No final name approved.

## User reaction and refined promise

Rally received a positive response: the user says it is pretty good. Their explanation emphasizes accelerating work, enjoying the process of making schedules, budgets and production plans, and making that work feel possible. Treat Rally as a favored reference, not a selected name. Keep human, creative, playful and friendly character and expressive visual potential.

Working creative synthesis (assistant wording, not approved tagline): **Move the production forward. Enjoy getting there.** Speed should feel enabling rather than pressuring.

## Adjacent names

| Name | Why it fits this territory | Difference from Rally / reservation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Riff | Creative play that becomes something real; short, energetic and human. Can cover schedules, budgets and production plans. | More expressive and improvisational; less explicit about coordination and dependable planning. |
| Scoot | Friendly movement: get going and move things along without making work feel heavy. | More playful and speed-oriented; could feel too small or cute for a substantial suite. |
| Tempo | Forward motion with a rhythm that makes the moving parts manageable. | More composed and less mischievous; musical/timing association must not make budgeting feel secondary. |
| Moxie | Confidence and resourcefulness: the production feels achievable. | More personality and can-do spirit than team coordination; familiarity and pronunciation may vary by audience. |
| Huddle | People coming together to make a plan and move forward. | Warmer and more collaborative, but evokes a meeting more than acceleration. |

Art-direction recommendation for the next comparison: retain Rally as the reference; compare Riff for creative energy and Scoot for friendly momentum. Tempo is a useful calmer alternative. These are creative recommendations, not user approvals or availability claims.

Illustrative spoken-product checks, not approved module labels: Riff Budgets / Riff Schedules / Riff Productions; Scoot Budgets / Scoot Schedules / Scoot Productions; Tempo Budgets / Tempo Schedules / Tempo Productions.

## Nearby ideas set aside

- **Sprint:** conveys speed but risks deadline pressure rather than enjoyment.
- **Hustle:** suggests grind and overwork, which conflicts with making the work feel doable and fun.
- **Rocket:** communicates acceleration but can feel overblown and less human.

These are subjective fit judgments, not market research or legal rejections.

## Current review question

User response: Rally and Huddle sound pretty good. These are the current favored candidates, superseding the assistant's suggested Riff/Scoot comparison. Neither is selected or availability-screened. The other options remain preserved as exploration.

Next creative question: should the identity lean toward Rally's forward energy or Huddle's sense of belonging? These associations are art-direction interpretations for discussion, not established audience research.
