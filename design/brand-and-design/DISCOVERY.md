The identity must represent that suite from the outset; schedule-only names are unsuitable. This does not authorize module implementation in the creative task.

- Preserve the schedule look accepted after B15 as a named, versioned, selectable Roseland preset covering schedule presentation in the editor, read-only views and print/PDF. Do not silently restyle existing documents. A separate Roseland interface preset is an open option.
- Keep product/interface identity, document appearance and operational content templates distinct. Appearance selections confer no data access or privileges.
- Preserve or improve familiar workflows, information density, field capacity, timing/grid behavior, navigation, editing, saving/recovery, sharing and intended output. Small productions get usable defaults; complex organizations can configure controls. Design does not reopen settled functional or permission policy.
- Phone/iPad usability, keyboard access, legibility and print/PDF output are design criteria. Representative outputs include schedules, call sheets and contact sheets.
- Name, logo, typography, palette, personality, visual hierarchy and exact customization controls remain open. Earlier concepts and unfinished research establish no approval or verified rights.
- Creative discussion and documentation proceed here. Devtest's work continues independently. Technical checks and explicit B15 functional acceptance precede reskin implementation; selected design approval is also required. B17 revalidates the final candidate; B18 separately authorizes migration.

## Proposed process

1. **Define the audience and promise.** Identify the primary launch customer, then the main benefit and desired personality. Ask one meaningful question per turn; reuse existing decisions and explain the consequence of each choice.
2. **Develop naming territories.** Use the agreed positioning to compare a small set of distinct naming approaches, then shortlist names with pronunciation, meaning and expansion rationale. Production Command remains a candidate only if useful. Record preliminary availability screening separately from legal clearance; registration or purchase requires separate authorization.
3. **Develop comparable identity directions.** After the user's positioning answers, research relevant products and inspect existing visual references before evaluating them. Prepare two or three distinct directions with wordmark/logo approach, typography, palette, interface tone and document styling. Compare each with the same fictional content and surfaces. Existing reference work need not dictate the new directions.
4. **Refine in context.** Compare editor, library/navigation, branding settings, client/read-only view and print/PDF examples, with a phone/iPad sample and preserved Roseland comparison once the accepted B15 baseline is available. Include normal and dense/long-content examples. Separate interface changes from document styling and demonstrate organization identity without sacrificing legibility. Iterate the selected direction before broad application.
5. **Prepare an approved design specification.** Save exact approved examples and asset versions, tokens, font/asset licenses, customization boundaries, responsive/output rules and remaining questions. Queue the specification through Voice and Planning for bounded implementation after the gates. Final implemented design review precedes B17.

This is a discovery proposal, not approval of its eventual designs or permission to implement. No external research, new concepts or visual evaluation has been performed in this kickoff. Older research was read as historical reference, without refreshing its web claims. Engineering documents contain older final-name and batch-status wording; the September 18 canonical naming correction controls. This task has not audited Devtest's current completion status.
