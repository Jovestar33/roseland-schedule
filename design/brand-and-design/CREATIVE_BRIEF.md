# Creative brief

## Product and users

**Framing confirmed by the user:** this is an identity exercise. Develop the product name, personality and visual language together, expressed through the existing schedule app and extensible to the broader production-management product.

**Confirmed personality:** human and creative, with the user's initial preference for bold and expressive. Apply this to naming and visual exploration. Specific names and visual executions remain unselected.

**Confirmed naming character:** catchy and fun; broad enough for the full production suite, including budgets and multi-day master scheduling.

**Personality refinement:** playful and friendly is the user's explicit choice over a bold/powerful naming personality. Keep the visuals bold and expressive while the brand feels approachable, human and creative. No specific visual execution is implied.

**Central promise:** make production work fun. The user reports that users say the existing app makes scheduling fun, and wants budgets and master scheduling to deliver that same experience. This is a substantive experience/identity goal, not merely playful decoration. First naming proposals are in [Naming round 01](NAMING_ROUND_01.md); no name is selected.

**User response to naming:** Rally feels promising because it suggests accelerating work and making schedules, budgets and production plans enjoyable and achievable. Explore friendly momentum and creative energy around that reference. [Naming round 02](NAMING_ROUND_02.md) records the next proposals. Rally is not a final selection.

**Latest naming feedback:** the user likes both Rally and Huddle. Keep both as favored exploratory candidates; neither is approved as the final name or screened for availability.

A commercial production-management product whose existing first capability is the daily schedule app. The user says budgets and master production scheduling across multiple days are coming very soon. Build the identity for that broader suite from the outset, encompassing scheduling, budgeting and production direction/management. Avoid schedule-only names. These are user-stated near-term intentions, not verified delivery dates, and do not expand this task into implementation. Production Command is a working name, not the final consumer brand.

Small casual productions should start work immediately with sensible defaults. More demanding organizations can configure controls. Visual design must support efficient information-dense editing, legibility and familiar workflows.

September 18 creative direction: prioritize beautiful, modern website and application design that appeals to Gen Z and even Gen Alpha. This task serves primarily as graphic designer, visual aid and art director. Technical checks belong to engineering/coordinating tasks, not this creative task. Generational appeal does not yet establish the primary launch industry or a specific visual style.

Further September 18 direction: the user leans bold and expressive and wants to develop a new umbrella name for the entire future product. Production Command feels very Gen X to the user; retain it only as the existing working label while exploring replacements. The name must accommodate scheduling and broader production management, rather than identify only the schedule app. No replacement is selected.

## Fixed requirements

- Company-neutral base interface, configurable organization name/logo/colors/fonts.
- Preserve or improve proven legacy functionality: grid/timing, field capacity, navigation, editing, saving, recovery, sharing and intended print/PDF output. Read existing behavior before asking the user to redesign it.
- Preserve the schedule look approved by the user after functional review as a named, versioned, selectable Roseland preset, available to users. Do not silently restyle existing documents.
- Evaluate a separate optional Roseland interface preset for the user's own organization. This is not yet selected and is not a new privilege.
- Keep interface appearance, schedule/document appearance and content templates distinct. Presets contain no private production data and grant no access rights.
- Future modules must fit the system, but are not implemented during this reskin.

## Order and deliverables

Early fictional-data hands-on preview is planned after B11 sharing, without new numbering. B15 remains formal technical/parity and user functional approval. B16 then preserves the approved look, develops/researches directions, refines the selected direction, applies it in bounded sections and completes final refinements. B17 revalidates; B18 separately authorizes migration.

When creative work is authorized, produce sourced research, naming options with rationale, comparable identity/design directions, editor/library/settings/client-view and print/PDF examples, then an approved implementation specification. Track fonts/assets and license status. No trademark/domain availability or rights are established by this brief. User approval precedes broad application.
