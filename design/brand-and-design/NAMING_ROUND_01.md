# Naming exploration — round 01

September 18, 2026. Creative proposals only. No user-approved name. These names have not been screened for existing products, trademarks, domains or social handles; inclusion is not an availability claim.

## Identity anchor

Make production work feel fun. The user reports that people using the existing app say it makes scheduling fun, and wants that quality to extend to budgets and multi-day master production scheduling. This is user-reported feedback and an intended brand promise, not an independently measured result.

Personality: human, creative, playful, friendly, catchy and fun. Visuals: bold and expressive, with appeal to Gen Z and Gen Alpha. Scope: the overarching production suite, not only daily scheduling. Product defaults remain company-neutral with organization branding and the preserved Roseland schedule preset.

## Names worth exploring

| Name | Creative rationale | Possible visual expression | Reservation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jamble | Suggests a creative jam and people making something together; bouncy, friendly sound with room for multiple modules. | A lively wordmark with shapes that come together; expressive color and a tidy interface. | Can sound like jumble or jumbled; test whether that evokes disorder. |
| Rally | Invites a team to come together and get something made; energetic and easy to say. | Warm, confident lettering with a gathering or meeting-point motif. | Familiar word with sporting associations; potentially difficult to distinguish in the market, pending actual screening. |
| Tada | Names the delight of getting something finished; makes an administrative tool feel celebratory. | An expressive wordmark and a restrained reveal/spark motif. | May feel gimmicky if celebration overwhelms practical use; pronunciation/spelling should be tested. |
| Tandem | Human and collaborative; people and moving parts working together. | Paired forms, rhythmic spacing and warm color relationships. | More reassuring than playful; may need stronger visual personality. |
| Goodtake | An optimistic production phrase with a friendly voice; can house schedules, budgets and other production tools. | Editorial lettering with a lively accent and subtle production cues. | More film-specific and less abstract than the other options. |

Initial art-direction preference: explore Jamble for personality, Rally for team energy and Tada for the fun-of-progress promise. These are recommendations for exploration, not approved names. Tandem and Goodtake provide useful comparison points.

Module-language checks are illustrative labels, not a product navigation decision: Jamble Schedule / Jamble Budget / Jamble Productions; Rally Schedule / Rally Budget / Rally Productions; Tada Schedule / Tada Budget / Tada Productions.

## Ideas set aside in this round

These are creative judgments, not legal rejections or newly approved user decisions.

| Name | Why set aside |
| --- | --- |
| Production Command | The user finds it too Gen X; its command-and-control tone conflicts with the desired playful, human personality. |
| Scheduleify | The user's illustrative non-fit: confines the umbrella identity to scheduling. |
| Budget Buddy | Friendly, but defines the suite around a single module and can suggest a personal-finance product. |
| Chaos Club | Playful, but celebrating chaos undermines the confidence a production tool should create. |
| ProdPal | Tries to signal production plus friendliness, but the abbreviation sounds awkward and technical. |

## Review

One question: which, if any, of Jamble, Rally, Tada, Tandem or Goodtake feels closest to the product's personality?

Record the user's reaction before refining or screening a shortlist. Disliking all five is useful direction; none should be treated as selected through silence or presentation alone.
